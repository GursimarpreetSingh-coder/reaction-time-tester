How to Play

  • Choose a game mode and start.
  • Wait for the target to appear before clicking.
  • Faster click = better reaction.
  • Early clicks count as invalid attempts.
  • Use settings to tune shape, size, color, and difficulty.
  • Press Space to start and Escape to close panels.
